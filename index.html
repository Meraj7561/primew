about.js

Whoami

PrimeWebHelix is a MERN-stack studio founded by Meraj Ahmad, focused on turning product ideas into fast, reliable web applications. We own the full cycle: data modelling in MongoDB, building resilient APIs with Express and Node, and shipping polished interfaces in React.

We care about clean architecture, readable code and interfaces that feel effortless to use — whether it's a solo build or a small team working alongside our clients.
